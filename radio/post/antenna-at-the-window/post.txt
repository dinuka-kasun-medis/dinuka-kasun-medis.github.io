For today's experiment, I used a broomstick to extend the antenna outside the window while it remained secured to my tripod setup.

Reception was clearer than in my previous indoor tests. I could hear the RSSL Daily Net on the Yatiyanthota 2m repeater, 145.625 MHz, and continued testing SDR++ settings.
